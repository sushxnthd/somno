# Delete your Somno account and data

Google Play requires this page to exist and to be reachable **without installing the app**, so that
somebody who has already uninstalled Somno can still get their data removed. It has to be linked
from the Play Console listing under "Data deletion", and it is linked from inside the app at
Settings → Data & privacy.

---

## If you still have the app installed

This is the fastest route, and it is immediate.

1. Open Somno.
2. Go to **Settings → Data & privacy**.
3. Tap **Delete**, and confirm.

That removes every row belonging to your account from the server, wipes the app's storage on your
phone, and cancels any alarms it had scheduled with the operating system — so a deleted account
cannot ring your phone afterwards.

There is no waiting period and no review. It is gone when the confirmation completes.

## If you have already uninstalled the app

Email **support@somno.app** from the address you signed up with, with the subject **"Delete my
account"**. Nothing else is needed — the address you write from is what identifies the account.

If you can no longer send mail from that address, say so and describe the account; we may ask
something only the account holder would know before deleting it, because deleting the wrong person's
sleep history is worse than a short delay.

**Timing:** deletion is normally done within one working day, and always within 30 days. You will
get a confirmation when it is done.

---

## What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account:

- The account and your email address
- Your check-ins — reaction times, face-scan measurements, ratings and alertness scores
- Every night you logged
- Your reaction-time baseline and your facial baseline
- Your alarms and the record of each time one fired
- Your sleep-debt history, profile details and consent log

Camera images are not in this list because they were never uploaded. The face scan measures frames
on the device and keeps only numbers; any cached image lives on your phone alone and goes when the
app's storage is wiped or the app is uninstalled.

## What that means for data on your device

Uninstalling Somno removes everything it kept on the phone. Uninstalling **does not** delete a
server-side account on its own — use one of the two routes above for that.

## If you want a copy first

Deletion cannot be undone, so export first if you want to keep anything: **Settings → Data &
privacy → Export** produces CSV and JSON containing your full history.
